About cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This policy covers cookies on valena.in and those set when you follow a Valena offer to a store. It should be read with the Privacy Policy.

Cookies in use

CategoryPurposeSet by
EssentialSign-in, form protection and security settings. Needed for the site to work.Valena
Affiliate trackingLet the store attribute your order to Valena so cashback can be recorded.Stores and affiliate networks

valena.in does not use advertising cookies. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information in order to deliver them.

Store and network cookies

When you click through, the store or its affiliate network sets a cookie on its own site under its own privacy policy. These typically last from one session up to about 30 days, depending on the store. Blocking or clearing them before you finish your purchase can prevent the order from being recorded.

Your choices

Browser settings let you block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may stop parts of Valena from working; blocking tracking cookies may stop orders from being recorded and cashback from being paid.

Changes

If we add other cookie types, such as analytics, we will update this page and request consent where required.
