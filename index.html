Online cashback, India

A little of every purchase, returned to you.

Stores pay Valena a commission when you shop through our links. Once a store approves your order, we pass a share of that commission back to you, paid to your UPI ID or bank account.

The model

Where your cashback comes from

Many online stores pay referral commissions through affiliate programmes. When your purchase starts on Valena, that commission is paid to us, and we share part of it with you. Your price at the store stays the same.

More about Valena

Before you shop

  • Cashback is worked out on the order value the store pays commission on, usually excluding GST, delivery and fees.
  • Cancelled and returned orders don't qualify.
  • Coupon codes from other websites can stop an order from being recorded.
