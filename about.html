About Valena

Valena is an online cashback platform for shoppers across India, run from Moirang, Manipur.

What we do

A share of the referral, back to the shopper

Online stores reward websites that send them customers. Valena lists stores with these affiliate programmes. When you buy through a Valena link and the store approves the order, the store pays us a commission and we return part of it to you as cashback.

Valena is not a seller. We don't stock products, take payment for your orders or handle delivery. Prices, shipping, returns and refunds are between you and the store.

How we earn

Commission, openly shared

Our income is the commission stores pay for referred sales. The cashback rate on each offer is the portion we pass to you. You pay the store's usual price, and where you shop is always your choice.

Store names and marks shown on Valena belong to their owners and appear only to identify where an offer applies.

How we run Valena
